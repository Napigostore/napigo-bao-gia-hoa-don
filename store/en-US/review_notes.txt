PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Quote & Invoice Maker is an easy-to-use invoicing and quoting app tailored for contractors, freelancers, and small businesses. It allows users to quickly generate professional quotes, sales receipts, and invoices directly on device without requiring server accounts.

HOW TO USE (no login, no account needed)
1. Open the app: no registration required. All data is saved privately on the local device (offline-first).
2. Select Document Type: create a new Quote or Receipt/Invoice.
3. Add Items: enter line items, quantity, unit price, discounts, and tax rates.
4. Customize Layout: pick from available professional templates and document styles.
5. Export & Share: generate clean, high-resolution PDF documents ready to share or print.
6. In-App Purchases: navigate to the pricing or plan upgrade screen to view available yearly subscriptions (Pro and Business) and tap "Restore Purchase" to restore previous purchases. Core document creation and PDF export features are fully accessible for free.
